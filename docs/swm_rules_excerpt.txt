GENERATOR DUTIES (Rule 4)
 • Segregate waste at source into: biodegradable / non-biodegradable (dry) /
   domestic hazardous. Hand over segregated waste to the collector.
 • Do not litter, throw, dump, or deposit solid waste on any public place,
   drain, water body, or park.
 • Permit door-to-door collection at the time fixed by the local body.

LOCAL BODY / OCCUPIER DUTIES (Rules 15, 16)
 • Door-to-door collection and non-littering of the jurisdiction.
 • Cleaning of water bodies and storm-water drains in the area.
 • Timely disposal; no putrescible waste left beyond 48 hours.

RELEVANT TO THIS SYSTEM
 • An unauthorised dump site is a rule contravention by the responsible
   authority, not merely a nuisance — the case is routed to the sanitation
   in-charge of the ward.
 • Penalties for contravention are levied by the local body (fine amounts are
   schedule-specific: demo excerpt — verify against the Gazette notification).
 • Dumping adjacent to a school or a water body raises the health risk and is
   prioritised for removal.
